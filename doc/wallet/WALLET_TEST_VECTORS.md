# Soqucoin Wallet Test Vectors

> **Version**: 1.3 | **Updated**: 2026-10-10
> **Status**: Specification
> **Audience**: Wallet Developers, SDK Implementers, Auditors

---

## Overview

This document provides deterministic test vectors for wallet implementations. An implementation of the seed
derivation or the address encoding MUST reproduce the vectors of sections 1, 2 and 6.

**State of each section.** Section 1 is the output of `src/test/pqderive-test --json-vectors` at `main`; the unit
suite `pqderive_seed_tests` pins five of its ten values (the signing key at path 0, the three blinding factors and
the channel funding key). Sections 2 and 6 are computed from the encoding rule in `src/utiladdress.cpp` and the
prefixes in `src/chainparams.cpp` (stated in `WALLET_CRYPTOGRAPHIC_SPEC.md` section 3), recomputed independently;
no unit test reads them. Section 3 is a placeholder for vectors that are not generated yet.

## 2. Address Encoding Test Vectors

> **Generated from**: `src/utiladdress.cpp` (`EncodeDestination`, `DecodeDestination`) and `src/chainparams.cpp`
> (`bech32HRP`) at `main`, 2026-10-10.
> **Rule**: bech32m (BIP 350); the human-readable part is the chain's prefix (`sq` on mainnet, testnet and regtest,
> `ssq` on stagenet); the data part is the witness version as one 5-bit value, `1` (the character `p`), followed by
> the 8-to-5 conversion of the 32-byte program; the program is the single SHA-256 of the 1,312-byte ML-DSA-44
> public key. A decoder accepts an address only when the encoding is bech32m, the prefix is the node's, the first
> data value is 1 and the program converts to exactly 32 bytes.

## 3. Transaction, cost and signature vectors

Not generated. The sections that stood here (transaction construction, verification cost, signature vectors and
the negative vectors for encryption, key generation and addresses) carried placeholder values and rules the code
contradicts, and are removed until real vectors are produced from the node; the work is tracked in the project's
tracker. Until then, the only normative statements for signatures, witness items and wallet encryption are those
of `WALLET_CRYPTOGRAPHIC_SPEC.md` sections 1.5, 3.4, 4 and 7.
